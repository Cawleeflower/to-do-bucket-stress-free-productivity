# ADR-012: Delegates don't use the app; they get a one-task link

- Status: Accepted
- Date: 2026-11-09
- Decided via Q4 (delegates reached through email, WhatsApp or Teams)

## Context

Users hand next steps to people who will never install the app (FR-05).

## Decision

Delegates don't use the app. Each delegation gets a private one-task link, sent by email from the app, or by WhatsApp and Teams as a ready-written message from the user's own account.

## Rationale

No sign-up for delegates; uses channels people already read; costs nothing; sync stays single-user.

## Trade-off accepted

Anyone holding the link can reply; WhatsApp and Teams sends need one tap from the user.

## Revisit when

The budget allows the WhatsApp Business Platform, or delegates ask for accounts.
